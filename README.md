## Get playing in 3 steps

**1. Download** &nbsp; Get `ttt2_setup.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/shihabal3amri/tekken-tag-tournament-2-pc/releases/latest).

**2. Point it at your game** &nbsp; Choose your disc image of *Tekken Tag Tournament 2 (USA, Europe)*
and its *Title Update 3*. The installer checks them and builds the PC version for you, usually
in a few minutes.

**3. Play** &nbsp; Launch *Tekken Tag Tournament 2* from your Desktop or Start menu.

> [!NOTE]
> Windows may say **"Windows protected your PC"** because the installer isn't code-signed.
> Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **You need your own copy of the game.** This download contains no game files: the art,
> sound, stages and movies are all built on your PC from your disc image and title update.
> They are never included here.

## Play online with friends

1. Both of you install the **same version** of the port and keep **Steam** running.
2. **Host:** `ONLINE MODE` → `PLAYER MATCH` → `CREATE SESSION`
3. **Your friend:** `ONLINE MODE` → `PLAYER MATCH` → `SEARCH SESSION`, then pick your session.
4. Fight!

The first time you open Online Mode, the game shows Namco's online terms; accept them to
continue. Steam will show you as playing **Spacewar**. That's normal: it's the free Steam test
app the port uses to connect players.

## Controls

**Controller:** works just like on Xbox 360. Whoever presses a button first is Player 1.

**Keyboard** (Player 1):

| Key | Does | Key | Does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arrow keys | Move / D-pad | `A` `S` `Z` `X` | A / B / X / Y |
| `Enter` or `Space` | START | `Q` `W` | LB / RB |
| `Backspace` | BACK | `E` `R` | LT / RT |
| `Alt` + `Enter` | Window ↔ full screen | `D` `F` | Left / right stick click |

**Change the keys** in the game: `OPTIONS` → `CONTROLLER SETUP` → on `BUTTON SETTINGS` press
left/right to switch it to `KEYBOARD`, then `A` to edit. Pick a button, press `A`, then press the
new key. `LB` / `RB` show the movement, START and BACK keys, plus `DEFAULTS` to undo everything.

Display mode and resolution are in the game's own menu: `OPTIONS` → `DISPLAY SETTINGS`.

**Input delay:** the original game adds 2 frames of delay to every button press in offline modes.
This port removes them by default; turn them back on in `OPTIONS` → `GAME OPTIONS` → `INPUT DELAY`.
Online matches always use the game's own delay, the same for both players.

## Questions

<details>
<summary><b>Is this an emulator?</b></summary>
<br>
No. The game's original Xbox 360 program was translated ("recompiled") into a real Windows
program, with new graphics, audio and online code written for PC. That's why it runs smoothly,
at high resolution, without an emulator.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why do I need my own copy of the game?</b></summary>
<br>
The port doesn't include the game's files. The installer builds them from your disc image and
title update, so you need to own the game to play.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where do I find Title Update 3?</b></summary>
<br>
On your own Xbox 360, title updates are stored in
<code>Content\0000000000000000\4E4D0859\000B0000\</code>. The one you need is
<code>TU_174Q22P_0000008000000.00000000000O2</code> (it ends in the letter <b>O</b>, then <b>2</b>).
You can also point the installer at the whole folder; it picks the right file by itself.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Which version of the game works?</b></summary>
<br>
The retail <i>Tekken Tag Tournament 2 (USA, Europe)</i> disc. The installer checks your disc
image and tells you if it's a different version or damaged.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>The first fight stutters for a moment.</b></summary>
<br>
The game prepares its graphics the first time you see them. After that they're cached and it
stays smooth.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>My PlayStation (or other) controller doesn't work.</b></summary>
<br>
The game reads Xbox-compatible controllers. Other controllers need a tool that makes them show
up as an Xbox controller, such as DS4Windows for PlayStation pads.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why are there two "Tekken Tag Tournament 2" in Task Manager?</b></summary>
<br>
The second one is a small helper that talks to Steam for online play. It closes together with
the game.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where are my saves? How do I uninstall?</b></summary>
<br>
Saves, profiles and settings are in <code>%LOCALAPPDATA%\TTT2Recomp</code>. To uninstall, use
<b>Settings → Apps → Installed apps</b>. Your saves are kept unless you tick the box to remove
them.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Something went wrong.</b></summary>
<br>
Open an <a href="https://github.com/shihabal3amri/tekken-tag-tournament-2-pc/issues">issue</a>
and attach <code>%LOCALAPPDATA%\TTT2Recomp\log.txt</code>. It tells us what happened.
</details>

## Not available (yet)

Online leaderboards, World Arena, replays and ghost downloads relied on Namco's servers, which
are gone. Voice chat isn't supported. Ranked matches and Steam invites ("Invite to Game") haven't
been tested yet.
